Privacy and cookies.

What this site collects, why, and how to change your choice.

Effective 13 September 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by JustDukkan, LLC, 1111B S Governors Ave, Suite 46654, Dover, DE 19904, USA. Questions about this page: enes@justdukkan.com.

What we collect

Server logs. The site is hosted on Vercel and served through Cloudflare. They process standard request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this data to identify you.

Analytics, only with your consent. If you accept cookies in the banner, we load Google Analytics 4. It records which pages you view, how you arrived, your approximate location (city level), and your device and browser type. We use this to understand which content is useful. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell data.

What you send us. If you email us or book a call, we keep that correspondence to reply and to prepare for the call.

Cookies and local storage

  • jd-theme (local storage): remembers your light or dark theme choice. Functional, no consent needed.
  • jd-consent (local storage): remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics. Functional, no consent needed.
  • _ga, _ga_* (cookies, Google Analytics): set only after you accept. They distinguish visitors and sessions and last up to two years.

Declining does not limit anything on the site. Analytics simply does not run.

Third parties

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): only after consent, as described above.
  • Cal.com: the booking calendar on the contact section is loaded from cal.com. When you book, Cal.com processes the details you enter to create the meeting. Their own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts: the site's font is loaded from Google's servers, which see your IP address to serve the file.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email enes@justdukkan.com. To change your analytics choice on this device, use the button below.

Changes

If we change what the site collects, we will update this page and the date above.
